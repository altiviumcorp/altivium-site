What We Build

A safety response layer for drones operating near people.

Altivium focuses on the moment when drone failure needs to become visible. The product direction is simple: detect dangerous descent, warn nearby pedestrians, and support safer evacuation before impact.

01

Fall-Risk Detection

Identify abnormal flight behavior that may indicate dangerous descent or loss of control.

02

Active Warning

Trigger clear warning signals so people nearby can recognize danger quickly.

03

Pedestrian Protection

Create a practical buffer of reaction time before the projected impact risk becomes immediate.
