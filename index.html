Vision

Giving people on the ground more time to react.

Altivium is a student-led startup focused on the safety layer required for wider industrial drone adoption. We believe unmanned flight can become part of everyday infrastructure only when failures are no longer silent. Our approach is to detect dangerous descent, activate immediate warnings, and support safer pedestrian evacuation before impact.

What We Build

A safety response layer for drones operating near people.

01

Fall-Risk Detection

Identifying abnormal flight behavior that may indicate a drone is entering a dangerous descent or loss-of-control scenario.

02

Active Warning

Activating visible and audible warning signals so people nearby can recognize danger quickly instead of discovering it too late.

03

Pedestrian Protection

Creating a practical buffer of reaction time so pedestrians can move away from the projected risk area before impact.

Market Direction

Targeting organizations that operate drones near real assets and real people.

Altivium is exploring opportunities with drone training centers, infrastructure inspection teams, logistics operators, and public safety organizations. These groups need more than flight performance; they need systems that make failure states visible, warn people on the ground, and make missions easier to approve.

Our early strategy is centered on customer discovery, pilot partnerships, and use cases where operational risk, approval cycles, and stakeholder trust directly affect drone adoption.

01 Industrial Operators Inspection, logistics, construction, and enterprise drone teams
02 Public-Sector Missions Municipal, safety, monitoring, and infrastructure programs
03 Training Ecosystems Professional drone education and field validation partners
